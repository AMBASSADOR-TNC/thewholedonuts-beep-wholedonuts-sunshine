Resonance Module

The Resonance Module measures how identity truth spreads through individuals, communities, and culture. It tracks emotional alignment, narrative adoption, and identity activation.

Resonance Metrics

  • Identity Echo — How often identity truth is repeated or referenced.
  • Emotional Alignment — The strength of emotional connection to identity.
  • Narrative Adoption — How many people integrate identity into their own story.
  • Activation Rate — How many individuals take action based on identity truth.
  • Spread Velocity — The speed at which identity moves through networks.

Purpose

Resonance reveals whether identity is alive, moving, and connecting. It is the heartbeat of the movement.
