Movement Protocol

The Movement Protocol expands identity from the individual level into community, culture, and global impact. It is how Whole Donuts becomes a living movement.

Movement Steps

  1. Ignition — A single identity truth sparks resonance in others.
  2. Connection — Individuals align around shared identity values.
  3. Formation — A community forms with unified emotional coherence.
  4. Expansion — The movement spreads through clarity, stability, and outreach.
  5. Identity Continuity — The movement maintains its truth across growth.

Outcome

The Movement Protocol produces identity expansion, cultural influence, and long-term Whole Donuts impact.
