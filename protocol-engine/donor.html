Donor Protocol

The Donor Protocol aligns supporters with the identity truth, mission, and movement of Whole Donuts LLC. It ensures donors understand the purpose, impact, and identity they are supporting.

Donor Alignment Steps

  1. Identity Presentation — Show donors the core identity truth of the movement.
  2. Purpose Clarification — Explain exactly what their support empowers.
  3. Emotional Connection — Build resonance between donor identity and movement identity.
  4. Impact Mapping — Show donors how their contribution creates real change.
  5. Ongoing Relationship — Maintain clarity, updates, and identity alignment.

Outcome

The Donor Protocol produces committed supporters, aligned identity relationships, and long-term movement stability.
