Ecosystem

Whole Donuts Ecosystem

The operating structure behind the movement: landing, identity, merch, and domain-routing nodes all work together as one network. This is the map that turns the mission into a system.

Service Architecture

  • Landing — wenevergonnaclose.com as the gateway and first impression.
  • Whole Donuts — wholedonuts.org, .app, .me, .pro, .buzz.
  • Merch — wholedonuts.store and thenutur3dchef.com for commerce and support.
  • Nurtured Chef — thenurturedchef.com and .foundation.

System Logic

  • Origin starts at the landing page and routes by domain identity.
  • Service detection maps hostnames to the correct operational lane.
  • Domain routing ensures each section behaves as part of a unified movement.
