Whole Donuts Ecosystem

The movement is built as a layered system: landing, commerce, domains, service logic, and identity all work together to produce continuity. This is the architecture that turns the story into a live operating network.

Service Structure

  • Landing — the public entry point and first impression.
  • Whole Donuts — the main brand and ecosystem lanes.
  • Merch — commerce and support flows tied to the movement.
  • Nurtured Chef — parallel identity layer with its own domain structure.

System Logic

  • Every site entrymaps to the correct domain identity.
  • Each ecosystem lane keeps its brand integrity without fragmenting the mission.
  • Back-end routing and public signaling align through the same central narrative.
