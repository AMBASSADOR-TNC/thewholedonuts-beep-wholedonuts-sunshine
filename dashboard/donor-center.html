Donor Center

The Donor Center manages supporter alignment, mission clarity, and identity resonance. It ensures donors understand the truth, purpose, and impact of Whole Donuts.

Donor Tools

  • Identity Alignment — Shows how donor identity matches movement identity.
  • Impact Tracker — Displays the real-world effects of donor support.
  • Support Channels — Lists active donor communication pathways.
  • Contribution Map — Visualizes donor influence across the movement.
  • Donor Protocol — Guides donor onboarding and long-term alignment.

Donor Purpose

The Donor Center ensures supporters feel connected, informed, and aligned with the identity and mission of Whole Donuts.
