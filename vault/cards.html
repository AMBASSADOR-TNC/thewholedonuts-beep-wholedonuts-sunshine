Identity Cards

Identity Cards are the core fragments of truth that define the Whole Donuts identity system. Each card represents a piece of emotional, logical, or metaphysical identity.

Core Identity Cards

  • Card 1 — Origin Truth Identity begins before the story is written.
  • Card 2 — Emotional Coherence Emotion becomes stable when identity is understood.
  • Card 3 — Narrative Alignment Your story must match your identity truth.
  • Card 4 — Path Formation Identity grows only when it moves.
  • Card 5 — Reconstruction Broken identity can be rebuilt through clarity.
  • Card 6 — Movement Identity Identity becomes powerful when shared.
  • Card 7 — Vault Permanence Truth becomes eternal once stored in the Vault.
