Identity Protocols

These protocols are the operational tools of the Whole Donuts identity system. Each one guides transformation, clarity, stability, and reconstruction.

Core Protocols

  • Clarity Protocol — Helps individuals understand their identity truth.
  • Stability Protocol — Builds emotional grounding and coherence.
  • Reconstruction Protocol — Rebuilds identity after disruption.
  • Outreach Protocol — Connects identity to community impact.
  • Donor Protocol — Aligns supporters with identity purpose.
  • Movement Protocol — Guides the expansion of the Whole Donuts universe.
